What this prototype can and cannot do

  • Can: open a user-mediated BLE device chooser, connect to a selected device, and inspect a limited set of permitted standard GATT services.
  • Can: show relative microphone input level while the page is open and microphone permission is granted.
  • Cannot: list every nearby Bluetooth device, discover Bluetooth Classic audio speakers, scan silently in the background, or reliably identify which device is producing the gym music.
  • Cannot: lower or switch off someone else's equipment. Any future control integration must use an authorized interface for equipment you own or have permission to manage.
A BLE device appearing in the chooser does not prove it is a speaker or belongs to the gym. Signal strength and device names can be incomplete or misleading.
